Questions, answered

The quick version, before we chat.

Who is Kavya Chawla?

A content strategist and social media manager based in New Delhi. I studied English Literature, and now I help founders turn their feeds into something people actually want to follow.

What services do you offer?

End-to-end social media management, Instagram reels and short-form video editing, content strategy (pillars, calendar and voice), and Instagram ad creatives like statics and carousels.

Do you work with brands in Delhi NCR?

Yes. I'm based in New Delhi and work with brands across Delhi, Gurugram (Gurgaon), Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Faridabad, and across India.

Are you a Gen Z social media manager?

Yes. I'm Gen Z myself, so I know what makes my generation stop scrolling: trends, reels and posts that sound like a person, not an ad. I help brands reach younger audiences without trying too hard.

What kind of brands do you work with?

Founder-led brands, coaches and small businesses. Past work includes The Quince Life, Elite Car and Coached by Shweta.

Can I see your work?

Yes. The Work page has case studies, and there's a portfolio PDF on this page. One reel for Coached by Shweta reached 620K views.

How do I hire you?

Fill in the form on the Contact page, and we'll book a short discovery call to see if we're the right fit.
